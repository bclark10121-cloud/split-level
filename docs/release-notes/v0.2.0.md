# Split Level 0.2.0: The look

Released 2026-11-22. Your house now looks like your house. Walls, floors, cabinets, counters, tile and carpet come from your photos. The light is warmer, the house hums and the black cat is in.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. You should see the new start screen: your front door open a crack from the top of the stairs, a warm glow in the gap, the cat on the landing. Music should start on your first click.
2. Press Play and walk up the steps. The den floor should look like your oak, the walls like your greige paint and the fireplace like your tile.
3. Walk into the kitchen. Check the cabinets, the quartz counters and the backsplash against the real thing.
4. Listen while you walk. Footsteps should sound different on oak, carpet and tile. The house hums softly, a little louder near the fridge.
5. Open doors until you meet the cat. She starts showing from door 5, in about one room in nine. Walk up to her and press E, A or tap Pet. She should purr and you should earn Here Kitty.
6. Open her door. The room behind it should be plain, calm and warm.
7. Note anything that looks wrong next to your house, with a screenshot.

## What is new

- Photo textures cut from your pictures on every room: oak floors and stair treads, greige, blue and lavender walls, cabinet fronts, quartz counters, the kitchen backsplash, fireplace tile, carpet, bath tile, laundry vinyl and the landing slate. No photo of a person or a personal item went into any texture.
- Warmer, softer light with a faint haze. Glass glows warm white. Nothing is dark.
- Room sound: a quiet room tone, a fridge hum that grows in the kitchen and footsteps that follow the floor under you.
- The black cat. From door 5 on she sits beside a door in about one room in nine. Her door always leads to a calm room. Pet her for a purr.
- Two new achievements: Here Kitty (pet the cat) and Nine Lives (nine cat visits). New stat: Cat visits.
- A new start screen of your own landing. The door swings a little wider, the glow grows and the cat looks up. Dust drifts in the light. Reduce motion stills it.
- Music. "Comfortable Mystery" on the start screen and "Clean Soul" quietly while you walk, both by Kevin MacLeod.
- The 2018 house change now shows carpet in the den and plain dark counters.

## Full test checklist

1. Start screen: the door, the glow, the cat and the title all show, upright and sideways on a phone.
2. Walk the whole upper level. Compare each room with your photos. Note colors that look off.
3. Stand at the top of the stairs and look down at the front door. The landing should be lighter oak than the upstairs floor.
4. Footsteps on oak, then in a carpet room, then on tile. Each should sound different.
5. Walk into the kitchen and the nook. The fridge hum should get louder.
6. Open doors to at least door 20. Did the cat show up? Was she ever in your way? (She never should be.)
7. Pet her three times in one room. Cat visits should go up by one, not three.
8. Turn on Reduce motion in Settings and go back to the start screen. It should hold still.
9. Install the APK on your phone. Are the textures sharp? Does it stay smooth when you turn quickly?
10. Turn the music down in Settings. Room sound should stay.

## Judgment calls

- Stair treads use the upstairs oak. The front landing uses the lighter foyer oak.
- The oak came out too orange next to your photos on the first try. I warmed it toward your honey color.
- Your rails are a darker stain than the game's. The game uses the same oak as the floor for now.
- Cabinet fronts stretch a little to fit each cabinet whole, with no cut-off pieces.
- The kitchen has no microwave, range knobs or sink details yet. The range and fridge are plain shapes.
- A room too small for the cat passes her to the next room, so she still shows about one room in nine.
- The fan shadow on the start screen is a soft overlay, since phones get no real shadows.
- The look and sound decision page is open. This build uses the Recommended picks. Your choices get swapped in next.
