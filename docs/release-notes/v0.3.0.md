# Split Level 0.3.0: Weirder

Released 2026-11-25. The deeper doors bend the house more and more. Plain keepsakes hide in the rooms, every room you find goes in a room book and three small goals wait each day. Your look and sound picks are in: a new icon, the Space Mono title and a new tagline.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page and press Play. The title should read Split Level in a plain typewriter-like font, with "Every door opens somewhere new." under it. Turn your phone upright: you should look down the stairs at the front door ajar, with the cat on the landing.
2. Start a walk and open doors. Around door 5 to 10 look for a small glinting thing on the floor or a counter. Walk up and press E, A or tap Pick up. You should hear a soft chime and see "Found:" at the top.
3. Open the Menu, then Room book. The rooms you found should be there with small pictures. Check the Keepsakes tab.
4. Open Records. You should see today's three goals.
5. Use Tester tools in Settings to jump to door 21, then door 36. The rooms should get stranger: a hall with twelve doors a side, five kitchen islands, a dining room upside down.
6. Tell me which room felt the most wrong in a good way, and which felt too much.

## What is new

- Three new stretches of doors. Doors 11 to 20 put real parts in the wrong rooms. Doors 21 to 35 stretch and repeat things. Doors 36 to 60 make the impossible: rooms inside rooms, the dining room upside down, stairs that land where they started, a dollhouse kitchen in the den. Past door 60 they stack two or three deep.
- 44 changes in all, up from 6, and four new rooms for the deep doors: a long hall, a room with a door on every wall, a stairwell and a big empty room. The dining room and the closet join the rooms too.
- 20 keepsakes: a spare key, a tape measure, a paint card, a light bulb, a mug, a brass bell and more. About one room in five from door 4 has one.
- The room book in the Menu: every room you find with a small picture, by stretch, and a Keepsakes tab.
- Three daily goals on the Records screen. No streaks. Nothing is lost if you skip a day.
- Gentle rooms in Settings leaves out the upside-down and strongest rooms.
- Four new achievements: First Keepsake, Twenty Keepsakes, Book of 25 Rooms and Door 60. Sixteen in all. New stats: keepsakes found and rooms in the book.
- Your picks from the look and sound page: the door-and-cat icon, the Space Mono title, the tagline "Every door opens somewhere new."
- Achievement and find notes now show on screen. They were hidden in 0.2.
- The start screen held upright now looks down the stairs at the door and the cat. Before, it showed only the stair rail.

## Full test checklist

1. Walk to door 60 the normal way. Did each stretch feel stranger than the last?
2. Was any room dark, too big to cross, or hard to find the way on? (None should be.)
3. Did anything ever hang low enough to feel like you would hit your head?
4. Pick up five keepsakes. Does each one show in the Keepsakes tab?
5. Open the Room book on your phone, upright and sideways. Can you scroll it with your thumb?
6. Turn on Gentle rooms and open ten doors past 36. Are the rooms calmer?
7. Close the game and come back. Are your book, keepsakes and goals kept?
8. Delete a slot in the slot list. Does a new game start with an empty book?
9. Install the APK. Check the new icon on your home screen.
10. Load your 0.2 save in slot 1. It should still continue.

## Judgment calls

- Look and sound picks: you said to go with my best judgment, so every Recommended pick on the page is in. You can still change any of them.
- Room size: no room is longer than 48 ft, and the nearest way on is never more than about 7 seconds away.
- Keepsakes show at 1.6 times life size so they read from eye height. Once you find all 20, no more appear.
- The book keeps a picture for the first 240 rooms in each slot. After that rooms still go in the book, with a "No picture" card.
- Finishing all three daily goals gives a note, nothing more.
- The back door "in the ceiling" is a door high on the wall at the top of a short stair. Doors stay on walls.
- The cat's calm rooms never hold a keepsake.
- The icon shows the cat at about twice her size in play and the door open wider than on the start screen, so both read at phone icon size. The icon scene keeps only the landing front, with plain side walls.
- Upright, the start screen camera stands back at the top of the stairs, so the cat looks small there. A closer view would put her under the title.
- A fridge magnet keepsake was swapped for another plain object, since your privacy rules rule out magnets.
- Tests check room brightness on sample rooms only, not across every seed. The deep rooms checked all came out as bright as the den or brighter.
