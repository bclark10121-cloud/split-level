# Split Level 0.1.0: The doors

Released 2026-11-22. The first test build. Gray box: plain colors and simple shapes. The real look comes in 0.2.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. Click once. You should be able to look around with the mouse.
2. Walk up the seven steps. You should see the den: vaulted ceiling, fan, fireplace, window and back door.
3. Turn right. You should see the dining room over the stairs behind the iron railing, and the kitchen past it.
4. Walk to the short hall on the left and open a bedroom door (E, A or tap Open). You should see a lavender bedroom.
5. Walk in. The door should shut behind you and the corner should read Door 1.
6. Open three more doors. From door 4 one thing should be off in each room.
7. Note any room that feels too big or too small.

## What is new

- Walk in the front door of a split level house in first person. Seven steps up to the den, eight steps down to the lower landing.
- The whole upper level stands to scale: the den with its vaulted ceiling, the breakfast nook, the kitchen, the dining room over the stairs and the short hall.
- Open any door and a new room waits behind it. Walk in and the door shuts behind you. Every door, the one you came in by included, now leads somewhere new.
- The first three doors open onto ordinary rooms. From door 4 on, one thing is off in every room: a fan hanging at head height, a kitchen with no island, a room flipped left to right, a floor of the wrong kind, a low ceiling or the house as it looked years ago. Past door 10 two things are off at once.
- The door count shows in the corner and on the range clock. Your best is the deepest door you reach.
- A run saves at every door. Continue picks up in the same room. Start over from the front door is in the Menu and keeps your best.
- Ten achievements, stats, five save slots, Settings with look speed, invert look, field of view and a center dot.
- Keyboard and mouse, Xbox controller and touch, in landscape and portrait.

## Full test checklist

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser. Click once to look with the mouse.
2. Walk up the seven steps. Look up at the vaulted den ceiling.
3. Do the rooms feel the right size? Note any that feel too big or too small.
4. Walk to the short hall and open a bedroom door (E, A or tap Open). Walk through.
5. Open doors until door 10. Did each room after door 3 have something off? Did you spot it?
6. Go back through the door you came in by. Is it a new room?
7. Open the Menu, Start over from the front door. Is your best kept?
8. Install the APK on your phone. Walk with the left thumb, look with a drag on the right half. How does it feel?
9. Try the Xbox controller: left stick walks, right stick looks, A opens.
